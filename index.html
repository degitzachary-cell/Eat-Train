Food database

—

The complete Australian Food Composition Database, Release 3 (FSANZ) is built in — every food, straight from the official nutrient profiles, per 100 g. The 213 foods FSANZ also publishes as liquids are held per 100 mL, which is the honest basis for a drink. Energy is the label figure, calculated with dietary fibre.

Add a packaged product

AFCD covers generic foods, not every supermarket line. For a branded product, copy its Nutrition Information Panel into New food. To bring in a whole list at once, load a CSV with a name column and any of energy, protein, fat, carbs, sugars, fibre or sodium — columns are matched by name.
